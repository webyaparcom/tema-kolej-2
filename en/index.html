Glossary

The parent’s glossary

Tap a term to highlight its questions.

Pre-registration2Guidance1Club1Photo consent1School bus1
Pre-registration01

How does pre-registration work?

You book a meeting day by WhatsApp or e-mail; the parent privacy notice is signed at the meeting.

Pre-registration02

Is pre-registration final enrolment?

No; final enrolment follows the meeting and depends on places.

Guidance03

What does the guidance teacher do?

Follows the class’s wellbeing, study habits and contact with parents.

Club04

Are clubs paid?

In-school clubs are part of the timetable; details are shared at the meeting.

Photo consent05

Are student photos shared?

Only with the parent’s explicit consent and only in the parent channel; this site shows no student photos.

School bus06

Is there a school bus?

Buses run on the Sehitkamil and Sahinbey routes (fictional).
